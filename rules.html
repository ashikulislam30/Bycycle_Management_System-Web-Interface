Rules & Regulations

  1. Secure Credentials: Keep login information confidential and do not share it with others.
  2. Adhere to Traffic Laws: Follow local traffic regulations and wear mandatory safety gear while riding.
  3. Timely Bicycle Return: Return bicycles within specified time limits to avoid additional charges.
  4. Respect Others: Respect pedestrians and fellow cyclists, and park bicycles in designated areas.
  5. Report Issues Promptly: Report any damage, malfunction, or theft promptly through the designated channels.
